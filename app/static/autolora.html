New voice from one recording

Upload one long recording of one speaker only, such as an audiobook chapter, a lecture or a voice memo. The server cuts it at the pauses into clips of 3–15 s, and the speech-to-text server transcribes each clip. You then check the transcripts and train. 10–30 minutes of clear speech works best; music, other voices and noise make the voice worse.

Review transcripts

The transcripts come from speech recognition and can be wrong, and the model learns exactly what they say. Listen to the clips (flagged ones first), fix any word that does not match the audio, and remove clips with other voices, music, laughter or cut-off words. A change is saved when you leave the text box.
